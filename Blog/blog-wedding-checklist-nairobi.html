Essential Checklist for Planning a Wedding in Nairobi

Shelter Sky Events · Nairobi, Kenya

Planning a wedding is one of the most emotional journeys in life. It involves excitement, anticipation and many decisions that must come together at the right time. If you are planning a wedding in Nairobi, this simple checklist will help you stay organised and confident throughout the process.

Start with a clear budget. This allows you to prioritise what matters most. Some couples value decor, others prioritise photography or food. When you know your budget, every decision becomes easier.

Next, choose your venue. Garden weddings remain popular in Nairobi because of the beautiful landscapes and natural lighting. Hotels and indoor halls are also great for couples who prefer controlled environments. Once you choose the venue, confirm the date and availability.

Select your decor theme. Whether you want something elegant, rustic, modern or culturally inspired, choosing a theme early helps you stay consistent. Share your ideas with your events team so they can plan the layout and style effectively.

Create your guest list. This determines the seating, catering and arrangement. Nairobi weddings range from intimate gatherings to large celebrations, so knowing your expected numbers guides every part of the planning.

Think about your ceremony setup. Walkways, floral arrangements, seating and backdrop design all influence the atmosphere. Simple elements like soft draping, warm lighting and fresh flowers create a timeless and romantic look.

Plan your reception arrangement. This includes tables, centrepieces, lighting and entertainment. Music, speeches and lighting all work together to create the mood for the evening.

Throughout this process, having a supportive events partner makes everything easier.
